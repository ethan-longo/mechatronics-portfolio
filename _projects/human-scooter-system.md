excerpt: Dynamic modelling and simulation of a two-wheeled transporter
---

## Overview
Modelled and analyzed a two-wheeled human transporter system similar to
an inverted pendulum. Simulations focused on maximizing stable travel
distance without feedback control.

## Tools
- MATLAB
- Simulink
- SimulationX
- System dynamics modelling
- Technical reporting

## What I Did
- Derived nonlinear equations of motion for the system
- Linearized the model for stability analysis
- Built Simulink state-space models
- Designed and parameterized 3D SimulationX models
- Ran experiments varying thrust forces and initial angles
- Analyzed results and contributed to final reporting

## Outcome
Achieved up to 16.5 meters of stable forward motion without a controller.
Demonstrated the necessity of feedback control for long-term stability
and laid groundwork for future closed-loop designs.
