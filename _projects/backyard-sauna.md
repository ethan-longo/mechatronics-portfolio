## Overview
Designed and constructed a custom backyard sauna from concept through
final build, balancing thermal efficiency, structural constraints,
and aesthetics.

## Tools
- SolidWorks
- AutoCAD
- Excel
- Hands-on construction
- DIY research

## What I Did
- Designed the full sauna layout and dimensions
- Created 2D layouts and 3D SolidWorks models
- Evaluated roof geometry for thermal efficiency and aesthetics
- Calculated internal volume and heating tradeoffs using Excel
- Researched materials and construction best practices
- Led all hands-on construction and material selection

## Outcome
Delivered a fully functional sauna that met all design constraints,
used cedar for thermal and moisture performance, and resulted in a
durable, efficient, and relaxing backyard space.
