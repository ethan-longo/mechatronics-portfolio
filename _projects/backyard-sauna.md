      <h3>Design Constraints & Requirements</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>
          <strong>Footprint:</strong> Limited to a 12′ × 8′ concrete pad to avoid
          permitting requirements
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Height:</strong> Maximum height of 9 feet to align with the
          adjacent roofline
        </li>
        <li>
          <strong>Functionality:</strong> Required both a sauna room and a
          separate change room
        </li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="project-text">
      <h3>Design & Engineering Process</h3>
      <p>
        The design process began with 2D layout exploration in AutoCAD, followed
        by detailed 3D modelling in SolidWorks to validate proportions,
        clearances, and roof geometry.
      </p>
      <p>
        After multiple layout iterations, a two-room configuration was selected:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>6′ × 8′ change room</li>
        <li>6′ × 8′ sauna room</li>
      </ul>
    </div>
  </div>

  <!-- Row 5: Engineering Details -->
  <div class="project-row">
    <div class="project-image">

           alt="Sloped roof construction detail">
    </div>

    <div class="project-text">
      <h3>Engineering Details</h3>
      <p>
        A single-slope roof was designed, ranging from a height of 7 feet on the 
        sauna end to a height of 9 feet on the change-room end. This geometry 
        reduced the internal volume to improve the heating efficiency while 
        maintaining visual continuity with the surrounding structures.
      </p>
      <p>
        Excel-based calculations were used to compare internal volumes, heating
        efficiency tradeoffs, and dimensional variations during the design phase.
      </p>
    </div>
  </div>
